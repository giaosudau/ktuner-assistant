# Downpipe, power and boost: condensed research

Condensed on 2026-10-09 from the downpipe-power research (branches `research/power-fundamentals`, `research/downpipe-effects`, `research/tuner-practice`, `research/ktuner-response-tables`, `research/front-af-sensor`, `research/fuel-slot-strategy`, `research/ignition-from-datalog`, `research/datalog-boost-method`, `research/boost-target-gap`, `research/log-evidence`) and the owner's KTuner 1.0.14.0 screenshots of 23 Aug 2026. It is the `source-doc` for the downpipe and boost knowledge cards.

Labels: **(vendor)** = a claim by the seller of a part or tune. **(10th gen)** = Civic X evidence, cross-check only. **(judgement)** = the shop's own call, not a published number. **(log)** = measured on this car's nine drives. **(screenshot)** = read from the owner's KTuner file.

## §1. Where power comes from

- Torque follows the mass of air trapped in each cylinder. Boost is a pressure, not a flow: the same psi with less exhaust back pressure traps more air. [Garrett Turbo Tech 103](https://www.garrettmotion.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/06/Turbo-Tech-103.pdf); [HP Academy, How to tune a turbocharged engine](https://www.hpacademy.com/technical-articles/how-to-tune-a-turbocharged-engine/)
- Fuel follows air. Best power on a boosted engine sits near λ0.82–0.85. The map asks λ0.75 (11.0 AFR) on purpose: richer cools the charge against knock and keeps turbine-inlet heat down; production turbo engines enrich to about λ0.73 at most. The car's measured λ0.69–0.73 is richer than asked: safe for knock and heat, and it costs a little power. Leaner full-load mixture for power is wrong on hot E10 RON95. [HPA, Choosing the correct AFR](https://www.hpacademy.com/courses/efi-tuning-fundamentals/air-fuel-ratio-choosing-the-correct-afr/); SAE 2018-01-0161 (SwRI / US EPA, 1.6 L turbo GDI)
- Spark is capped by knock before MBT on pump fuel. More boost on knock-limited fuel forces timing out: about the same power, more heat. [HPA, What is MBT?](https://www.hpacademy.com/technical-articles/what-is-mbt/); ORNL/TM-2016/42
- Honda's ECU is torque-based: the pedal is a torque request; the ECU sets boost and throttle, capped by the factory torque limiter (which protects the CVT and can pull boost) and by Cylinder Fill Limitation. Tuners describe Cylinder Fill Limitation ("knock air limit") as a knock safeguard that closes the throttle, not the everyday cap; the torque limiter is the next limit after the boost table. KTuner does not expose the torque limiter for the 22+ 1.5T. A downpipe makes the same air cheaper to get; it does not lift these caps. [Hondata 10th-gen Civic notes](https://www.hondata.com/help/flashpro/10g_civic_notes.htm) (vendor, 10th gen); [CivicX, Knock airflow limit table](https://www.civicx.com/forum/threads/knock-airflow-limit-table.36689/); [CivicX, Did KTuner modify the CVT torque limit table?](https://www.civicx.com/forum/threads/did-ktuner-modify-the-cvt-torque-limit-table.40687/); Bosch ME7, SAE 980801

## §2. What a downpipe changes

- Lower turbine outlet pressure brings boost in sooner. On 10th-gen dynos torque came 200–400 rpm earlier (27WON, PRL, Vit Tuned; 10th gen). No 11th-gen boost-at-rpm data exists. [27WON design blog part 2](https://www.27won.com/blog/downpipe-design-blog-part-2); [civicxi PRL thread](https://www.civicxi.com/forum/threads/prl-motorsports-downpipe-front-pipe-pre-order-1-5t-civic-x.8633/)
- Power without a tune (vendor): 27WON 200-cell on an 11th gen, +12 whp / +13 wtq peak, about +8 / +9 average, on the factory ECU; its first published figure was +7 / +11. So +7 to +12 whp. No independent 11th-gen dyno; TSP and RV6 publish none. [27WON 11th-gen downpipe](https://store.27won.com/2022-civic-turbo-performance-downpipe-11th-gen.html); [civicxi 27WON launch thread](https://www.civicxi.com/forum/threads/11th-gen-honda-civic-downpipe-from-27won-launches.50212/)
- No Honda tuner changes the map for a downpipe on the stock turbo: KTuner Starter maps, TSP Map 1/2/3 and Phearable levels are one file per car, dyno'd on unmodified CVT cars; a downpipe is "compatible", never "required". See §4.
- A downpipe changes nothing on fuel or spark: the full-load target stays 11.0 and timing is untouched (shop decision from the above, ticket 06 D3).
- Overshoot on an unchanged map: about +0.9 psi (10th gen); catless 10th gens on unadjusted maps spiked up to about 4 psi. The shop's only map action is a small boost trim where a log proves held overshoot (judgement; no tuner describes or contradicts it). [civicx 24 psi after downpipe](https://www.civicx.com/forum/threads/24psi-after-downpipe-install.41481/)
- (log) This car's nine drives were all recorded with the downpipe and front pipe already fitted (`docs/research/tuner-play-panel.md`, owner row). At the highest boost logged, up to 19.9 psi, the wastegate sat about 2.5 % open (2.4–3.7 % at full throttle from 3,500 rpm): the pipe opened no real turbine headroom. No drive reached 21 psi.

## §3. Catalyst codes, sensors and the Vietnamese inspection

- Sensor location: the front A/F sensor (Honda 36531-64A-A01, "SENSOR, LAF", catalogue note "Upstream. 1.5L turbo, to turbocharger") threads into the turbocharger outlet and does not move with a downpipe. Only the rear (secondary) O2 sensor sits in the downpipe; 27WON's 11th-gen install guide moves only that one. [hondapartsconnection 36531-64A-A01](https://www.hondapartsconnection.com/oem-parts/honda-oxygen-sensor-3653164aa01); [27WON install guide L15-6-209-15](https://www.performanceinstalls.com/Guide/L15-6-209-15+11th+Gen+Downpipe/175)
- A turbo-to-downpipe flange leak sits after the A/F sensor and ahead of the cat and rear O2: front reading and trims stay clean, the rear O2 reads falsely lean, and the catalyst monitor can set P0420. [Identifix, P0420 diagnosis](https://www.identifix.com/blogs/p0420-code-the-guide-to-diagnosis-and-fast-fixes/)
- Codes by class: 200-cell (27WON) sets P0420 for most owners within days (vendor: "CEL can be illuminated"); 400-cell GESI pipes (TSP, RV6 22+) claim no CEL and owners report none; catless always sets it. Older RV6 pipes were 300-cell. An O2 spacer is hit-and-miss and can swap P0420 for a slow-response P1039. [civicxi 27WON O2 CEL](https://www.civicxi.com/forum/threads/27-won-downpipe-oxygen-sensor-is-tripping-emissions-light.51165/); [TSP HE downpipe](https://www.twostepperformance.com/products/high-efficiency-downpipe-upgrade-for-2022-honda-civic-1-5t-2023-acura-integra-1-5t-2018-honda-accord-1-5t); [RV6 GESi downpipe](https://rv6-p.com/products/rv6-high-temp-catted-downpipe-for-22-civic); [civicxi RV6 P1039](https://www.civicxi.com/forum/threads/rv6-catted-downpipe-and-p1039-cel.52487/)
- Catless over a good catted pipe: about 0–6 whp (owner and vendor reports), exhaust smell in the cabin. [civicx 92701](https://www.civicx.com/forum/threads/catless-downpipe-recommendations.92701/); [civic11forum catless](https://www.civic11forum.com/threads/catless-downpipe.1357/)
- (screenshot) KTuner Main Parameters → Disables/Monitors: this car keeps P0420 enabled; P2096/P2097 (post catalyst too lean / rich) and P0128 are disabled.
- Vietnam: from 1 Mar 2026 (QCVN 85:2025) a petrol car gets a tailpipe CO / HC / lambda test plus a check that the MIL lights at key-on and goes out after start; 2022+ cars: CO ≤ 0.5 %, HC ≤ 300 ppm. No source says inspectors read OBD monitors. An unapproved structural modification carries a 65–75 M VND fine for a person (NĐ 168/2024). [tapchixaydung, petrol-car test procedure](https://giaothong.tapchixaydung.vn/quy-trinh-kiem-tra-khi-thai-xe-o-to-chay-bang-xang-theo-quy-dinh-moi-183290000000000590.htm); [luatvietnam, QCVN 85:2025](https://luatvietnam.vn/giao-thong/quy-chuan-ky-thuat-quoc-gia-qcvn-85-2025-bnnmt-khi-thai-xe-o-to-tham-gia-giao-thong-duong-bo-408747-d3.html); [otosaigon, NĐ 168 fine](https://www.otosaigon.com/threads/loi-do-xe-o-to-tu-y-thay-doi-ket-cau-co-the-bi-phat-toi-150-trieu-dong.10031025/)

## §4. What tuners change, and vendor files

- KTuner Starter 21 claims up to +55 whp / +58 lb-ft (vendor). TSP Stage 1 non-Si: 154 → 191 whp, Map 3 = 24 psi only, "slightly limited to avoid risk of damage to the factory engine and transmission" (vendor). Phearable Level 3 ≈ 23–24 psi (vendor). KTuner and TSP dynos share the IMW watermark and baseline: one source. [KTuner 22+ Civic 1.5T](https://ktuner.com/22civicturbo/); [TSP Stage 1 non-Si](https://www.twostepperformance.com/products/tsp-stage-1-tune-for-2022-honda-civic-1-5t-non-si); [Phearable Stage 1.5 non-Si](https://www.phearable.net/tuning-software/11th-gen-civic/11th-gen-civic-non-si/stage1-5-nonsi-11thgen.html)
- Every vendor asks for US 91 or better. Downpipe-specific gains come only from a custom e-tune that edits ignition and boost together.

## §5. Ignition, octane, the percent idea and fuel in Vietnam

- Honda's rule (KTuner and Hondata): final timing = the lesser of the Ignition table and table + knock limit − Knock Control × knock retard table. The ECU never runs more than Ignition Base; better fuel only gives back retard. Under boost on E10 RON95 this car sits at the 0.49 Knock Control floor, so the table is the timing. [KTuner, Ignition timing and knock control](http://www.ktuner.com/KTunerHelp/ignition_timing_and_knock_control.htm); [Hondata, Knock control tables](https://www.hondata.com/help/flashpro/knock_control_tables.htm)
- (screenshot / data) `Ignition_Base_H` holds 150 negative cells, all in the high-load columns. In the boosted region (3,000–6,000 rpm, top 8 load columns) cells run −6.4° to +4.7°, median −1.5°. A +5 % multiply moves that median cell about −0.08° (less timing) and adds about 1.05° in cruise cells (median 21°) that are already near ideal. +2 % adds about 0.42° there.
- All tuners work in degrees, add only with dyno proof and keep 2–3° below knock onset; on the street they remove timing where knock shows. [HPA, Practical reflash: ignition timing](https://www.hpacademy.com/courses/practical-reflash-tuning/basic-tuning-ignition-timing); [CivicX, Pro Civic on Knock Control](https://www.civicx.com/forum/threads/high-knock-control-reading-after-ktuner-v2-flash.19473/)
- Octane: Honda calibrates knock control between RON 90 and 100, "approx 85 and 95 octane r+m/2", so RON95 ≈ US 90 AKI (Hondata), 90–91 by the usual RON–MON estimate: at or just under the 91 every vendor asks for. [Hondata, Knock control tables](https://www.hondata.com/help/flashpro/knock_control_tables.htm)
- Above about 5,200 rpm the non-Si ECU raises Knock Control on purpose, so a fuel comparison reads it at or below 5,200 rpm only. On cool drives both fuels read the 0.49 floor; RON97 can only win in heat, at most the ≈ 1.6° that Knock Control 0.65 costs. A gap of 0.5° or less is inside the logging step. [CivicXI, Controlling knock control (IMW)](https://www.civicxi.com/forum/threads/controlling-knock-control.41116/page-2); [civic11forum K-Con](https://www.civic11forum.com/threads/k-con-knock-control.6222/)
- Fuel on sale (5 Oct 2026): Petrolimex lists E10 RON95-III, E10 RON95-V and E5 RON92 only; mineral RON95 and RON97 left the market on 1 Jun 2026; E10 RON97-III is sold by smaller retailers. [Petrolimex](https://www.petrolimex.com.vn/); [VietNamNet, 1 Jun 2026](https://vietnamnet.vn/tu-hom-nay-1-6-xang-e10-ban-dai-tra-dung-luu-hanh-xang-ron95-ron97-2520897.html); [S.T.S Petro E10 RON97-III](https://stspetro.com/ten-san-pham-05)

## §6. KTuner settings: tunes, dual boost targets, Quick Adjustments, the logged target

- On The Fly Map Switching: three tunes; they switch "between different boost targets and responsiveness levels"; ignition, fuel, knock and the throttle setting are shared by all three. Switch with a long press of the cruise Cancel button; switching while moving is supported. With the device unplugged the car starts in Tune 1. [KTuner, On the fly map switching](http://www.ktuner.com/KTunerHelp/on_the_fly_map_switching.htm); [CivicXI, KTuner questions for JR](https://www.civicxi.com/forum/threads/ktuner-questions-for-jr-idle-speed-expanded-quick-adjustments.34408/)
- Dual Boost Targets With ECO Button: on the Civic 1.5T the ECU uses Boost Target ECO in ECO mode and Boost Target Normal in Normal (Sport uses Normal). Starter 21 Dual Tune 2 = 18 psi ECO, 21 psi Normal / Sport. [KTuner, Dual boost targets](http://www.ktuner.com/KTunerHelp/dual_boost_targets.htm); [KTuner 22+ Civic 1.5T](https://ktuner.com/22civicturbo/)
- No TunerView channel records the tune or the ECO state; the owner tags them when attaching a log.
- (screenshot) Quick Adjustments on this car: Throttle Responsiveness = Factory Throttle Response CVT; Boost Target Ramp Rate = Factory Ramp; Tune 1/2/3 Turbo Responsiveness = Factory Responsiveness; Tune 1/2/3 Normal and ECO Boost Target = Custom Boost Level (the Boost Target tables). Quick Enables: Early Spool off. As digitized, the three tunes hold the same boost targets (Normal 21 / ECO 18); the screenshot shows the dropdowns, not the cells.
- What the levers do (KTuner Help, written for the 10th-gen platform; 22+ "Turbo Ramp Options" added in 1.0.11.9): Throttle Responsiveness changes plate opening for the same pedal, not torque; Boost Target Ramp Rate shortens the factory time ramp on the target; Turbo Responsiveness spools faster to part-throttle targets; Early Spool adds low-rpm boost. [Throttle responsiveness](http://www.ktuner.com/KTunerHelp/throttle_responsiveness.htm); [Boost target ramp rate](http://www.ktuner.com/KTunerHelp/boost_target_ramp_rate.htm); [Turbo responsiveness](http://www.ktuner.com/KTunerHelp/turbo_responsiveness.htm); [Early spool](http://www.ktuner.com/KTunerHelp/early_spool.htm); [Version history](http://www.ktuner.com/KTunerHelp/version_history.htm)
- Telling lag apart (judgement on the method of [Hondata turbo notes](https://www.hondata.com/help/flashpro/honda_turbo_notes.htm)): on a floored pull, time from pedal (TPS) ≥ 85 % to boost ≥ 90 % of target, read with EWG Position. Target ramping slowly while the wastegate is open = software ramp (one Quick Adjustment level up can help, one change at a time). Wastegate shut and boost lagging = physical spool (no setting helps). Early Spool stays off: it adds low-rpm torque where CVT and LSPI risk sit.
- (log) The logged boost target never exceeded 19.2 psi against a 21 psi table: the factory throttle dampening plus the time ramp hold it. It follows the pedal and climbs about 1–2 psi/s; the owner's pulls were part-pedal and short. No table caps it (Final Boost Target passes through, ceiling 23.4; Boost By Gear Limits flat 35.4; ethanol adjust 0). A +1 psi table edit shows only on a pull with the pedal floored and held ≥ 4 s above 3,500 rpm. [KTuner, Disable boost target dampening](http://www.ktuner.com/KTunerHelp/disable_boost_target_dampening.htm); [KTuner, Final boost target](http://www.ktuner.com/KTunerHelp/final_boost_target.htm)

## §7. Heat: IAT Ignition

- (screenshot) KTuner Ignition Corrections → IAT Ignition. "IAT Ignition High Load (Retard)": 0° at 35 and 40 °C, 4.5° at 50 °C, 9° at 90 and 100 °C. "IAT Ignition Low Load (Retard)": 0° at 35 and 40 °C, 1° at 50 °C, 3° at 90 and 100 °C. "Apply IAT Correction To Overall Timing" is off, so the correction rides in the knock limit (as `kc-ignition-formula` notes); it can take up to that much timing as intake air heats, on top of the Knock Control rise heat brings on E10 RON95.
- (log) Hot traffic raised Knock Control 0.49 → 0.64 on the 30 Aug 16:01 drive; a matched pair half an hour apart lost 3.2 psi of peak boost and 0.46 s on the timed run (`kc-heat-soak`).

## §8. The Boost step method (judgement)

Decided in tickets 06 and 16 from the tuner methods above (HP Academy, Hondata, IMW/TSP: one small step at a time, repeated pulls, boost read with the wastegate, knock read as a trend, pulls right after a Flash or while heat-soaked thrown out). The numbers are the shop's judgement; no Honda tuner publishes them. Sources: [HPA, 6 steps to tuning your turbocharged engine safely](https://www.hpacademy.com/technical-articles/6-steps-to-tuning-your-turbocharged-engine-safely/); [Hondata, Honda turbocharger notes](https://www.hondata.com/help/flashpro/honda_turbo_notes.htm); [Hondata, Tuning the K20C FK8](https://www.hondata.com/tech-tuning-the-k20c-fk8-civic-type-r); [CivicX, TSP on pulls after a flash](https://www.civicx.com/forum/threads/hands-on-with-tsps-stage-1-tune-for-the-1-5t-non-si.39135/page-13).

- Step: +1 psi on Boost Target 2 Normal (Low and High cam), rows 3,500–4,500 rpm, never above the active map's Final Boost Target peak (23.4 psi on this map). Written to Tune 2 so the owner can switch back to Tune 1 at once; a proven step is copied to Tunes 1 and 3. 24 psi is a vendor file (TSP Map 3 / Phearable L3), not a step.
- Proof pulls: pedal (TPS) ≥ 85 % held ≥ 4 s above 3,500 rpm; ≥ 2 pulls on the latest Cool drive, plus one hot drive.
- Gates, all of: logged target moved to the new value; undershoot ≤ 1.5 psi, read with EWG; overshoot ≤ 1.5 psi and target + overshoot ≤ 24 psi; timing no more than 1° below the last proven Map version; Knock Control settled after the Flash, read ≤ 5,200 rpm, no rise vs the last proven version, ≤ 0.56; AFR never leaner than 12.0; DIFP ≥ 90 % of DIFP Target; trims within ±5 % (log validity).
- The first failed gate undoes the step to the last proven Map version.
- Today (log): the hot drive's Knock Control peaked at 0.64 and no floored pull exists, so the ladder is closed. On E10 RON95 (≈ 90 AKI) expect it to stop early.
