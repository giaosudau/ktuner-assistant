Owners of the same engine read IAT2 differently: on some ECUs it is the air after the intercooler, on others the air before it. An owner showed a Southeast Asian ECU reading it after the intercooler, matching another tuning tool, while another owner reports before. So the shop never assumes which side IAT2 is on for this car. Until it is confirmed, intercooler health is not judged from IAT2 alone. A clue in your own log: air before the intercooler, straight off the turbo, runs far hotter on a long pull than air after it.
