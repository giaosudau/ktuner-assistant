The Fuel-quality score reads 0.49 at its best, and higher means timing is pulled everywhere. Watch starts at 0.56. No hard driving starts at 0.62 held over 60 seconds. Stop starts at 0.80, which this car has never reached. Under boost each point above the floor costs 10.2 degrees, so 0.56 costs about 0.7 degrees, 0.62 costs about 1.3 degrees, 0.65 costs about 1.6 degrees, and 0.80 would cost about 3.2 degrees. The 5.0 degrees of retard logged under boost at 0.49 is scheduled from the score, not knock events. Cool drives end at 0.49 to 0.53, and the hottest traffic drive stayed below the 0.66 maximum seen on this car. The cut points are judgement, labelled as such, not measured limits.
