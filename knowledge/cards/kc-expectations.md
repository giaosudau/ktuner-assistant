The Starter 21 map asks 18 psi in ECO and 21 psi in Normal and Sport, and the file tops out at 23.4 psi of final target. These logs were measured with a downpipe and front pipe already fitted: at the highest boost logged, up to 19.9 psi, the wastegate sits about 2.5 percent open, against 8.0 percent at idle and cruise and 65.5 percent at its most open. Nearly all exhaust already drives the turbine, so more boost mostly means more heat, not more power. KTuner claims the map adds up to 58 lb-ft, or 79 Nm, over a stock car rated 240 Nm and 176 hp. Gains from here come from cooler air and good habits, and more boost only from a step proven on your logs. Most drives end with no map change, and that is an answer, not a gap.
