A downpipe moves the front oxygen sensor into a new pipe, and the catalyst code often follows a few drives later. In KTuner the switch lives under Main Parameters, then Disables and Monitors, at the P0420 catalyst efficiency entry. Switching it off is an inspection risk, not a tuning step: an OBD inspection can see the disabled monitor, and the car fails đăng kiểm emissions testing with it off. Prefer a high-cell-count catted pipe or an oxygen sensor spacer first, check both flanges for leaks with the engine cold, and keep the monitor switched on wherever the car must pass inspection.
