A downpipe carries the rear oxygen sensor, the one the catalyst check reads; the front A/F sensor threads into the turbo outlet and does not move. So a downpipe flange leak shows on the rear sensor and the catalyst code, not on fuel trims. Most 200-cell pipes set P0420 within days; owners of 400-cell pipes report none. An oxygen sensor spacer is hit-and-miss: it can swap P0420 for a slow-response P1039. In KTuner the switch lives under Main Parameters, then Disables and Monitors, at the P0420 catalyst efficiency entry, and this car keeps it on. The đăng kiểm inspection tests tailpipe gas and checks that the warning lamp works; switching the code off hides a fault and does nothing for the gas test. Prefer a 400-cell catted pipe, check both flanges for leaks with the engine cold, and keep the monitor on.
