Every drive started soon after another one begins with 57 to 64 °C in the manifold. Cool drives read 35 to 39 °C while moving, and a pull should start at or below 48 °C. The intercooler is not the problem: under boost it adds only 2 to 6 °C over the inlet air. Two drives half an hour apart prove it: pull-start intake 49 °C against 60 °C, peak boost 19.9 psi against 16.7 psi, and the timed run 1.77 s against 2.23 s. After a hot stop, drive three to five calm minutes before any hard acceleration, and judge power only between drives at similar intake temperatures.
