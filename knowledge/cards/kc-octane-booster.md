Don't add octane booster. KTuner staff, a vendor and the long-running owner reliability thread all advise against it, and one engine failure in the largest owner census lists booster as a possible cause. If you suspect the fuel, try a different busy station, or E10 RON97, and compare tank against tank by Knock Control on matched drives.
