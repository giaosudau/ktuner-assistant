The 400-cell catted pipes from TSP and RV6 claim no warning light, and most owners report none, though a few see the rear-sensor code P2A01, or a light only in heavy rain or on long highway runs: that is still the shop's pick. The 200-cell 27WON pipe sets P0420 for most owners within days. Catless makes about 0 to 6 whp over a good catted pipe (owner and vendor reports), brings exhaust smell into the cabin, always sets P0420, and risks the đăng kiểm tailpipe gas test; an unapproved modification can also be fined 65 to 75 million VND. The tune response is the same for all three: no map change, and a small boost trim only if a log proves boost holds above target. Ask which pipe is fitted: older RV6 pipes were 300-cell.
