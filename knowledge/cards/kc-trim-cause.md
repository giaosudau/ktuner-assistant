- trim-stop: 10 %
- trim-ok: 5 %
- undo-trim: 15 %
---
Trims beyond 10 percent in every load band, from the first minute, right after a Flash or a fitted part, usually mean the airflow preset does not match the intake housing. Trims 15 percent or worse everywhere after a change mean Undo first: flash back the earlier file, then pick the preset for the housing. Trims wrong only at idle and low airflow, fine higher up, usually mean unmetered air, a leak after the sensor: check clamps and flanges with the engine cold, no map change. Never bend the airflow curve to cancel a preset mismatch, and never touch full-throttle targets for a trim fault. Prove it with a calm drive and trims within 5 percent.
