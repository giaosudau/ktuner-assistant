Read the whole log before touching anything, and remember which cells you already changed so no cell is corrected twice from the same evidence. Change one thing per round: fuel or airflow first, log again, and only then look at spark. Apply only part of what the log suggests, then let the next log show the rest. Judge with the engine fully warm, from mid-gear pulls, never first gear, and roll into the throttle instead of stabbing it. Never add timing on the street. After hard logging, let a turbo engine idle a minute before switching off. A Knock Count blip alone can be noise; repeated evidence decides.
