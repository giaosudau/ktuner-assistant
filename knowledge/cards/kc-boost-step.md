A Boost step adds 1 psi to Boost Target 2 Normal, Low and High cam, in the 3,500 to 4,500 rpm rows, never above the map's Final Boost Target peak, 23.4 here. Only the second tune gets it, so switching to the first undoes it; a proven step is copied to the others. Offered when the latest Cool drive holds two floored pulls (pedal 85 percent or more, held 4 seconds above 3,500 rpm) and a hot drive passes too. Gates: the logged target moved; undershoot and overshoot each within 1.5 psi, target plus overshoot at most 24; timing within 1 degree of the last proven version; Knock Control settled, not rising, at most 0.56 below 5,200 rpm; mixture never leaner than 12.0; fuel pressure at least 90 percent of target. The first failure undoes it. Today it is closed: the hot drive reached 0.64, no floored pull yet.
