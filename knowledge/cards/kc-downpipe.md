A downpipe lowers the back pressure after the turbo, so the same boost arrives a little sooner; on Civic X dynos torque came 200 to 400 rpm earlier. The parts vendor claims +7 to +12 whp with no tune; no independent dyno exists for this car. Your logs were all recorded with the downpipe and front pipe fitted, so that gain is already in them. No Honda tuner changes the map for a downpipe on the stock turbo, and nothing changes on fuel or spark: the full-load target stays 11.0 and timing is untouched. The one possible map action is a small boost trim, and only if a log proves boost holds above target. After fitting, check both flanges for leaks with the engine cold: a leak shows on the rear oxygen sensor and the catalyst code, not on the front A/F sensor.
