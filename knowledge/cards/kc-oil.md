Use the Honda manual grade 0W-20, rated API SP or newer: those ratings pass an engine test that averages five or fewer early-ignition events per run and never more than eight, while plain older grades require no such test. Lugging means 900 to 1,700 rpm with the foot down, and 31 of all 41 score rises on this car happened there, so the case against it is data, not theory. Below 60 km/h in traffic, use S or a paddle before pressing past a third of the pedal, and let it rev to 2000 rpm or more first. Its top end touches the 1,500 to 2,500 rpm low-speed early-ignition band, which is a second reason to stay out of it.
