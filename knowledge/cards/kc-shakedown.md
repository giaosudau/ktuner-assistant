After any Flash, drive calm for 10 minutes before any hard acceleration: steady speeds, no pulls, engine fully warm. Watch the trims first and the score second. Hard driving waits until a calm stretch shows trims within 5 percent; that is what proves the new map measures air correctly. Save the earlier file under its own name before flashing, so Undo is one flash back. If the trims never settle, or a new Stop appears on the Shakedown itself, flash back and say so: the Flash is not credited until a drive proves it.
