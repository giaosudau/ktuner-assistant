Log it like a shop asks. Before you drive: in TunerView turn on every gauge the app reads (AFR Command and MAF Hz included) and set the fastest rate it offers, 10 Hz or more. Go before 8 am or after an hour parked in shade, so the intake is under 42 °C. Drive 10 minutes normally until coolant is above 80 °C. Cruise steady at several speeds for trims. Then, on a straight, empty, legal road, in S: from a 50 km/h roll press the pedal fully and hold it to 100 km/h, then lift. Cruise a minute to cool and do the second pull, starting with intake under 48 °C. Keep at least 10 minutes of moving time in the log, then export the CSV.
