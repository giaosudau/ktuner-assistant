Non-Si ECUs raise Knock Control on purpose above about 5,200 rpm. Starter and base maps can switch this off with a checkbox, while some locked tunes keep it. Those rises say nothing about fuel quality or heat, so they are excluded from fuel and heat verdicts. This owner rarely revs past 5,200 rpm, with only two rises on one drive up there. A rise at high revs alone never opens a step and never changes a Verdict. Judge the tank by what the score does in normal driving and in traffic, where heat and lugging are the real movers.
