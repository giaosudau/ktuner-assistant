After every new Flash the Fuel-quality score starts high, around 0.58, and falls back to the Baseline with calm driving. A start at or below 0.60 that settles toward 0.49 is what a fresh flash does. It is never an Unexplained change, and it never sets a Verdict on its own. Give it ten to fifteen calm minutes before judging fuel or heat, because the score needs that long to relearn. If the score does not settle, or if it climbs again in traffic, that later rise is the finding, judged on its own. The Shakedown drive exists for exactly this pattern.
